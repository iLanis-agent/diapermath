Diaper Math

The 6200-gram baby at 150 grams a week reaches the 8000-gram size line in 12 weeks and 0 days.

Boundary: arithmetic only Diaper Math plans a diaper supply with addition and calendar counting: when the weight reaches the next size line, the diapers that takes, the packs to buy with what they cost, and the size-up date. It uses no named constants. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your baby, your scale or your store and does not know whether the gain will hold, a size truly fits or the price will stay, and it gives no verdict that a weight is healthy or a size is right. Every number here is your own entry.

Starting examples: a 6200-gram baby gaining 150 grams a week, the next size at 8000 grams, 8 diapers a day, packs of 44 at 1150 cents, starting 2026-10-24.

The diapers truth

At 8 diapers a day, the 84 days take 672 diapers.

The packs truth

The 672 diapers make 16 packs of 44, the last pack holding 12 diapers, costing $184.00, $0.27 a diaper.

The date truth

Starting 2026-10-24, the size line lands on 2027-01-16, a Saturday.

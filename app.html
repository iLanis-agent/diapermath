Diaper Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page plans a diaper supply with addition and calendar counting: when the weight reaches the next size line, the diapers that takes, the packs to buy with what they cost, and the size-up date. It uses no named constants. The weight, the weekly gain, the size line, the pace, the pack and its price are yours to enter from your own scale and your own store. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your baby, your scale or your store and does not know whether the gain will hold, a size truly fits or the price will stay, and it gives no verdict that a weight is healthy or a size is right. Every number here is your own entry.

The baby

Weight and gain in grams. The size line is where the next size starts.

The supply

The daily pace, the pack and its price in cents.

The size truth

Size: the weeks and days until the weight reaches the line.

The diapers truth

Diapers: the daily pace multiplied by those days.

The packs truth

Packs: the count, what the last pack holds, the cost and the price of one diaper.

The date truth

Date: the start date plus those days.
